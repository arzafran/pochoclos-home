Pochoclos Home privacy policy

Pochoclos Home is a home screen for Android TV. It has no accounts, no analytics, no ads, and no servers of its own. It does not sell or share personal data.

Weather. To show the local weather, Pochoclos Home asks IPinfo for the approximate city of your internet connection, which IPinfo works out from your IP address. It then sends that city's coordinates to Open-Meteo to get the forecast. Nothing else is sent, and the result is only kept on the TV.

Screensaver photos. When the Pochoclos app is not installed, the screensaver loads freely licensed photos from Wikimedia Commons.

Pochoclos content. If the Pochoclos app is installed and signed in, Pochoclos Home shows artwork from your own media server. It reads this from the Pochoclos app on the TV and never sends it anywhere else.

On the TV only. Your app order, hidden apps and when you last opened each app are stored on the TV and never leave it.

Home screen setup. If you choose to make Pochoclos Home your home screen, it connects to the TV's own debugging service, on the TV itself, to turn the stock home screen off. The connection never leaves the TV.

Accessibility service. The optional accessibility service only notices when the stock home screen opens, so Pochoclos Home can open instead. It does not read screen content and sends nothing anywhere.

Questions: use the contact email on the Google Play listing.
